How Budget Enforcement Works

THRESHOLDS

Defaults 0.5 / 0.8 / 0.95 fire one alert each per month — recorded in the Alerts tab.

WARN

At the warn threshold the gateway auto-routes to the agent's cheaper fallback_model.

HARD CAP

With hard_limit=true, requests are rejected with HTTP 402 once the budget is exhausted.
